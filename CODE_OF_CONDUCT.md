# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback, protect private information, and assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publication of private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. This document is a concise project adaptation of the Contributor Covenant principles.
